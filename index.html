Pause is a goal-tracking app built around one idea: you check in on your goals the same way you check in on yourself - slowly, on purpose, without the noise of a feed.

What it does

Three habits, one screen.

01

Set a goal that means something

No template, no streak pressure by default - just what you're actually working toward, in your own words.

02

Check in, briefly

A short daily or weekly pause to log progress. It's designed to take under a minute, not become another feed to scroll.

03

Watch the shape of your progress

Your history builds into a quiet, honest picture - not a leaderboard, just a record for you.

How it works

The loop

1

Create a goal

Give it a name, a reason and however much structure you want around it. Pause doesn't force a format on you.

2

Get a gentle nudge

A check-in reminder arrives when it's time - not a notification storm, just a tap to open the app and reflect.

3

Log it, honestly

Mark how it went. Progress, a setback, or nothing at all - the app tracks the truth, not a highlight reel.

4

Look back

Over weeks and months, your check-ins become a timeline you can actually learn from.

Where the build stands

Pause started as a web app and is currently being rebuilt as a native Android app, with a Room database handling the data layer across several screens.
